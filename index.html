Privacy Policy – Glow Garden

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alternative Reality Studio ("we", "us") handles information when you play Glow Garden (the "Game", Android package com.alternativerealitystudio.glowgarden). Glow Garden is free to play and supported by advertising.

In short: the Game does not ask for your name, e-mail address or any account. Your progress is saved only on your device. Our advertising, analytics and attribution partners collect device and usage information automatically, as described below. You can review or change your privacy choices at any time with the Privacy button in the Game's Gardens menu.

1. Who is responsible

Alternative Reality Studio is the publisher of the Game and the data controller for the information described in this policy. You can reach us at alternativerealitystudiomain@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, e-mail address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location. If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, settings such as sound and vibration, and your current puzzle) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Information collected automatically by our partners

When you play, the software development kits (SDKs) of our partners listed in section 4 automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers: the mobile advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS where you allow tracking), app-set or vendor identifiers, and identifiers generated by the SDKs;
  • Device and network information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size, mobile carrier, IP address and the approximate location (country or region) derived from it;
  • App and usage information: app version, install source and the advertising campaign that led to your install, session start and length, and gameplay events such as starting, completing or leaving a level, hints used and settings changed;
  • Advertising information: which ads were requested, shown or clicked, rewarded ads watched, and related ad revenue;
  • Diagnostic information: crash and error logs and performance data.

We do not collect special categories of personal data (for example health or religious information).

3. How we use information

  • to run the Game and keep it working on your device;
  • to understand how players use the Game (for example, which levels are too hard) and to improve it, including testing different versions;
  • to show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch, and, where you have agreed or where the law allows, ads personalised to your interests;
  • to measure our marketing: which campaigns lead to installs, and how many players they bring;
  • to detect and prevent fraud, abuse and technical problems;
  • to answer your messages and requests, and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some uses of advertising identifiers by ad partners may count as "sharing" or "selling" under certain U.S. state laws; see section 8.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • Consent screen: where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the Game shows a consent screen before personalised advertising and related measurement start. This screen is provided by AppLovin and integrates Google's certified consent management.
  • Change your choices any time: open the Gardens menu in the Game and tap Privacy. This opens the consent screen again.
  • Device settings: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID, which stops apps from using it for personalised ads. On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Consent for personalised advertising and for analytics, attribution and advertising measurement that relies on device identifiers. You can withdraw consent at any time with the Privacy button.
  • Legitimate interests for keeping the Game secure, preventing fraud, fixing errors and showing non-personalised ads that keep the Game free.
  • Legal obligation where we must keep or disclose information by law.

7. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK, Brazil's LGPD or U.S. state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Because the Game has no accounts, we can only identify data linked to your device. To make a request, e-mail alternativerealitystudiomain@gmail.com with the subject "Glow Garden privacy request", tell us what you want us to do, and include your device's advertising ID if you can (Android: Settings → Google → Ads). We answer within 30 days and pass deletion requests on to the partners in section 4 where they hold data about your device. You can also contact those partners directly.

Delete your data: uninstalling the Game removes all game data stored on your device. To ask for data held by our partners to be deleted, send us a request as described above.

8. Notice for U.S. residents

In the last 12 months the Game's partners collected the categories described in section 2: identifiers (such as advertising IDs and IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (gameplay and ad interactions), approximate geolocation and device information. These are used for the purposes in section 3. Allowing ad partners to use advertising identifiers for cross-context behavioural advertising may be considered "sharing" or a "sale" under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act. You can opt out by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your device settings (section 5) or by e-mailing us. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.

9. Data retention

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game. Our partners keep the data they collect for as long as their own policies describe, usually no longer than needed for attribution, analytics, ad delivery and fraud prevention. We keep e-mails you send us only as long as needed to handle your request.

10. International transfers

Our partners may process information in the United States and other countries outside your own. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Children

Glow Garden is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by law in your country, such as 16 in some EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

We choose partners that protect information with appropriate technical and organisational measures. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or our partners change. We will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the Game.
